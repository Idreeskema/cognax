Signing in

How do I sign in?

Go to the sign-in page and use your email and password, or continue with Google. If you forgot your password, choose “Forgot password?” on that page.

Files and the Knowledge Base

Where do I find my files?

Files and folders are in the Knowledge Base. Use the filters for status, domain and tags to narrow a long list.

How do I keep important files handy?

Star the files you use most. Starred files are easy to filter back to when the library grows.

Asking Naxie

Why is Naxie not answering from my document?

Check that the document is in the Knowledge Base and its status shows as ready. Documents that are still processing may not be available yet.

Can I pick up an earlier conversation?

Yes. Past conversations stay saved, so you can open one and continue it.

How do I check an answer?

Each answer shows where it came from. Open the source and read the passage yourself before you rely on it.

Dashboards and presentations

How do I build a dashboard?

Create a connection to your spreadsheet, create a dashboard, then add a report that uses the connection.

How do I make a presentation?

Pick a connection and create a presentation. Cognax generates the first draft from that data, and it appears in your list of decks.
