When does the supplier contract renew, and how do we stop it?
It renews for 12 months unless you give 90 days’ written notice before the end of the term. Supply Contract, section 9.3

Supply Contract, 9.3 Renewal

…shall renew for successive periods of twelve (12) months unless either party gives ninety (90) days’ written notice of non-renewal…
